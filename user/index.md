# User Guide

![Audience](https://img.shields.io/badge/audience-All%20Users-3ecf8e?style=flat-square)
![Login](https://img.shields.io/badge/requires-DSpace%20account-blue?style=flat-square)

Welcome to the **DSpace CRIS Cockpit** — a web interface for managing and browsing your institution's research information. This guide walks through every feature available to logged-in users.

## What You Can Do

<div class="service-grid">
  <div class="service-card">
    <h4>🗂 Workspace</h4>
    <p>Track in-progress and submitted items. Check validation status, continue drafts, and monitor workflow.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="service-card">
    <h4>🔍 Search</h4>
    <p>Full-text search across all published items with facet filters to narrow results by type, date, author, and more.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="service-card">
    <h4>🏛 Communities</h4>
    <p>Browse the repository hierarchy — communities, sub-communities, collections — and view items within them.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="service-card">
    <h4>🔗 Quicklinks</h4>
    <p>Preset search shortcuts for common entity types. Availability depends on your institution's configuration.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="callout callout-info">
<span class="callout-title">Not all features may be visible</span>
Your institution may have disabled certain features (e.g. Quicklinks) or restricted them to administrators. If you expect access to something that isn't visible, contact your repository manager.
</div>
